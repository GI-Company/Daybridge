# DayBridge Privacy Statement

**Effective Date:** October 2026

## 1. Single-User Personal Scope
DayBridge Personal Edition is an individual daily-life management tool. It is not an enterprise patient portal, healthcare provider software, or electronic court monitoring system.

## 2. Information Handled
- **Obligations & Schedule:** User-entered appointments, work shifts, counseling sessions, and court accountability classes.
- **Self-Reported Attendance:** Attendance logs created and owned solely by the user. Attendance is never described as officially or externally certified.
- **Health & Medications:** Personal prescription logs, dose reminders, and optional inventory estimates.
- **Budget & Cashflow:** User-entered manual balances, bills, and wage advances in pure integer cents without bank account credentials.
- **Direct Contacts:** Personally saved phone numbers and notes for coordinators, counselors, and crisis services.

## 3. Privacy Guarantees
- **No Third-Party Access:** Case managers, probation officers, judges, healthcare providers, and employers have no direct or backdoor login access.
- **Self-Reported Exports:** Weekly reviews and summaries are shared only if the user explicitly prints or exports a document. The user has the option to redact medication names before generating export reports.
- **No Commercial Data Brokerage:** Your personal data is never sold, licensed, or shared with commercial entities, insurers, or data brokers.
- **Generic Notifications:** Push reminders never reveal sensitive health or legal subjects on your phone's lock screen.
