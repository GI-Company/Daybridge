# DayBridge Assignment Summary & Friction Reduction Report

## Product Mission
DayBridge Personal Edition was designed for individuals navigating accountability court requirements alongside unpredictable daily life (work shifts, therapy sessions, medication regimens, and constrained budgets).

## Measurable Cognitive Friction Reductions

1. **The 5-Second Homepage Answer:**
   Instead of browsing multiple apps or navigating complex submenus, opening DayBridge immediately answers:
   - *What do I have to do next?* (Clear card with arrival buffer countdown, prep items, and map link).
   - *Did I take my scheduled medication?* (One-tap Taken / Snooze / Skip).
   - *What can I safely spend?* (Conservative Safe-to-Spend in integer cents, protecting essential gas, grocery, and bills before payday).
   - *What must I prepare for tomorrow?* (Top 3 tasks and tomorrow preview).

2. **One-Tap Routine Actions:**
   Every recurring daily action (logging a dose, checking off attendance, paying a bill, or checking off a preparation item) requires only one or two taps. Tap targets meet or exceed 44x44px.

3. **Wage Advance & Payday Trap Protection:**
   Earned wage advances (EarnIn, Dave, Brigit) are frequently miscalculated, leading people to believe they have more money on payday than remains. DayBridge automatically links advances to the upcoming paycheck and deducts them immediately, preventing double-counting of spendable cash.

4. **Non-Judgmental Respectful Language:**
   Eliminates coercive streaks, punitive colors, or shame-based notifications. If a medication dose was not recorded, it is marked as "No entry recorded" rather than an assumed non-compliant missed dose.
